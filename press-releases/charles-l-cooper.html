Charles L. Cooper Marks More Than Four Decades of Leadership in Financial and Retirement Planning

The Masters Financial Group leader Charles L. Cooper has built a career spanning more than 40 years helping individuals, professionals, business leaders and retirees navigate financial planning, retirement, estate strategies and insurance.

The Masters Financial GroupGreeley, COJuly 23, 2026

GREELEY, Colo.: The Dynamo Times highlights Charles L. Cooper for a financial-services career spanning more than four decades and characterized by long-term client relationships, financial education and a commitment to helping individuals prepare for their financial futures.

Mr. Cooper leads The Masters Financial Group, a financial-services firm with locations in Greeley, Colorado, and Grandville, Michigan. The firm works with clients nationwide and focuses on areas including estate and retirement solutions, tax-efficient investment strategies and insurance planning.

Its clientele includes individuals with significant assets, established professionals, business leaders and retirees seeking strategies designed around their individual circumstances and long-term objectives. For Mr. Cooper, financial planning has developed into a career centered on helping clients make informed decisions during some of the most consequential periods of their financial lives.

Mr. Cooper has provided financial guidance for more than four decades. During that time, the financial landscape has changed dramatically: markets have evolved, technology has transformed investing, retirement expectations have shifted, and tax laws and financial products have continued to change.

Yet one fundamental aspect of financial planning remains consistent: clients need professionals capable of understanding their goals and helping them evaluate complex financial decisions. Mr. Cooper has built his career around that relationship.

Retirement planning represents a particularly important component of Mr. Cooper's work. For many individuals, retirement is the culmination of decades of employment, saving and investment. Transitioning from accumulating assets to relying upon those assets can introduce an entirely different set of financial considerations: income, investment risk, taxes, insurance, estate planning and the preservation of assets.

Mr. Cooper and The Masters Financial Group work with clients to consider these issues as part of broader financial strategies. Estate planning can also become increasingly important as individuals accumulate wealth, establish businesses and consider what they ultimately want to leave to their families or other beneficiaries.

The Masters Financial Group also focuses on investment and insurance strategies. Investments can serve multiple purposes within a financial plan, from long-term growth to income and wealth preservation. Insurance can address a different set of risks and planning objectives. Bringing these areas together can allow financial professionals to examine a client's needs from a more comprehensive perspective.

Mr. Cooper has continued to develop his professional expertise throughout his career. He has earned the Certified Financial Planner (CFP®) certification and has qualified for membership in the Million Dollar Round Table (MDRT), an international association within the life insurance and financial-services profession.

Mr. Cooper's professional journey did not begin in the financial industry. Before entering financial planning, he worked his way through college as a railroad brakeman with Grand Trunk Railroad. He attended Michigan State University, where he studied criminal justice and psychology.

Following his education, Mr. Cooper initially pursued a career in law enforcement, working with the Greeley Police Department before making a significant professional transition in 1983. That year, he left law enforcement and entered financial planning: the beginning of a career that would ultimately span more than four decades.

His background in psychology and law enforcement provided experiences centered on understanding people, communication, responsibility and decision-making: qualities that remain relevant when working with individuals facing important financial decisions.

Education has become another component of Mr. Cooper's professional work. He has hosted seminars addressing subjects such as estate and investment planning, providing participants with opportunities to better understand financial concepts. He has also contributed articles to his local newspaper.

Financial education can be particularly important because many individuals encounter complex financial decisions without extensive formal training in investments, retirement planning, taxation or estate strategies. By sharing professional knowledge beyond individual client meetings, financial professionals can help make these subjects more accessible to a broader audience.

Outside of his professional activities, Mr. Cooper remains involved in his local community through participation in his church and volunteer work supporting charitable organizations. Beyond work, he enjoys spending time with his family, traveling, fishing, working on cars and motorcycles, and following sports.

Since entering financial services in 1983, he has worked through changing markets, economic cycles, technological developments and shifts within the financial industry. Throughout those changes, his work has remained focused on helping clients understand their options and develop strategies aligned with their objectives.

The Dynamo Times recognizes Charles L. Cooper for his longstanding contributions to financial services and for a career characterized by experience, education, leadership and long-term client service.

About The Masters Financial Group

Charles L. Cooper leads The Masters Financial Group, a financial-services firm with locations in Greeley, Colorado, and Grandville, Michigan, serving clients nationwide. With more than 40 years of experience, his professional work encompasses retirement and estate solutions, investment planning and insurance strategies. Mr. Cooper earned degrees in criminal justice and psychology from Michigan State University before beginning his career in law enforcement. In 1983, he transitioned from the Greeley Police Department into financial planning. He holds the Certified Financial Planner (CFP®) certification and has qualified for membership in the Million Dollar Round Table (MDRT).
