Kristina M. McGahan, RN, BSN, Advances Nursing Leadership, Clinical Mentorship and Professional Resilience in Health Care

With more than 28 years of cardiovascular nursing experience, McGahan has built a career that extends from bedside care into clinical coordination, precepting, consulting and the founding of Mind Over Matter: Nursing Recovery Summit.

KMM Consulting / Mind Over Matter: Nursing Recovery SummitMagnolia, TXSeptember 18, 2026

MAGNOLIA, Texas: Dynamo Times recognizes Kristina M. McGahan, RN, BSN, for a career that has evolved from frontline cardiovascular nursing into a broader mission of clinical leadership, professional mentorship and systemic change within health care.

With more than 28 years of nursing experience, McGahan has developed a perspective that extends well beyond any single clinical role. Her career has taken her from bedside cardiac and telemetry nursing through clinical coordination, precepting, consulting and, most recently, the founding of a recovery coaching initiative designed to address one of the most pressing challenges facing the nursing profession today: burnout.

McGahan's clinical foundation was built in cardiovascular environments where nurses must combine technical precision with rapid decision-making. Cardiac and telemetry units demand a particular kind of professional readiness: the ability to recognize subtle changes in a patient's condition and communicate those observations clearly and quickly. Years of experience in these settings gave McGahan not only clinical expertise but also an understanding of the pressures that shape a nurse's daily professional life.

Her role as a clinical nurse coordinator for a cardiac care unit placed her at the intersection of patient care and organizational leadership. Coordinating a clinical unit requires managing competing priorities, supporting staff, maintaining quality standards and ensuring that patients receive consistent, attentive care. That experience deepened her understanding of how leadership functions within health care, not as an abstract concept, but as a daily practice with direct consequences for patients and colleagues.

McGahan's work as a preceptor and educator reflects a commitment to the next generation of nursing professionals. New nurses enter clinical environments carrying academic knowledge but often lacking the confidence that comes from experience. Preceptors bridge that gap: they translate textbook learning into practical skill, model professional judgment and help newer nurses develop the resilience required for a demanding career. McGahan has described this mentorship work as one of the most meaningful aspects of her professional life.

Her book, What Every Nurse Must Know: Telemetry Secrets, published in October 2025, represents a natural extension of that educator identity. Telemetry interpretation is a skill that many nurses find challenging, particularly early in their careers. By distilling decades of cardiac nursing experience into an accessible resource, McGahan created a tool that can help nurses across the country build confidence in an area of clinical practice that directly affects patient safety.

The founding of Mind Over Matter: Nursing Recovery Summit reflects McGahan's recognition that the nursing profession faces a sustainability challenge. Nurses who entered health care with a deep sense of purpose can find that purpose eroded by chronic stress, understaffing, emotional exhaustion and the cumulative weight of caring for others through difficult circumstances. McGahan's coaching work does not ask nurses simply to endure more: it asks them to examine the patterns that have developed over time and consider whether those patterns are sustainable.

Her approach draws on her own professional experiences, which gives her coaching work a credibility that purely academic training cannot replicate. She understands what it means to push through exhaustion because patient needs are immediate. She understands the culture of nursing that can make it difficult to acknowledge personal limits. And she understands that recovery is not a sign of weakness but a prerequisite for a long and meaningful career.

McGahan's recognition (Nurse of the Year at Christus Santa Rosa Hospital in both 2021 and 2022, a Woman of Empowerment Award in 2025, and Pinnacle Lifetime Member status from The Inner Circle) reflects the breadth of a career that has touched clinical excellence, educational leadership and professional advocacy.

Looking forward, McGahan plans to expand her consulting and coaching work, reaching more nurses with education surrounding burnout recovery, resilience and professional growth. Her future work builds upon more than 28 years of clinical experience and a clear conviction: that strengthening the nurses who deliver care is inseparable from strengthening the care that patients receive.

Dynamo Times recognizes Kristina M. McGahan, RN, BSN, for her leadership in nursing education, her advocacy for nurse wellness and her sustained commitment to the professionals who form the foundation of health care.

About KMM Consulting / Mind Over Matter: Nursing Recovery Summit

Kristina M. McGahan, RN, BSN, is a registered nurse, nurse consultant, recovery coach and nursing educator with more than 28 years of health care experience based in Magnolia, Texas. She is the founder of Mind Over Matter: Nursing Recovery Summit and the author of What Every Nurse Must Know: Telemetry Secrets. Her clinical background spans cardiovascular care, cardiac and telemetry nursing, progressive care and medical-surgical nursing. She earned her BSN from The University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston in 1998.
