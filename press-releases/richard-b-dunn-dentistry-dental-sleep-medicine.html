Richard B. Dunn, DDS, FACD, Builds a Distinguished Career in Dentistry, Dental Sleep Medicine and Professional Leadership

Elmira, New York dentist Richard B. Dunn, DDS, FACD, has built a career spanning general dentistry, dental sleep medicine, implant education, professional leadership, public health service and continuing education.

Chemung Family DentalElmira, NYSeptember 22, 2026

Dynamo Times highlights Richard B. Dunn, DDS, FACD, a dentist whose professional record spans decades of patient care, continuing education, dental sleep medicine, professional association leadership and service to the dental community.

Dr. Dunn has practiced in Elmira, New York, and has been associated with Chemung Family Dental. A 2018 Continental Who's Who announcement recognized him as a Pinnacle Lifetime Member in the field of dentistry, while subsequent professional profiles documented his continuing work in dentistry and dental sleep medicine. His career reflects an approach to dentistry that extends beyond routine oral care to include sleep-related breathing disorders, professional education and leadership.

Dr. Dunn earned his Bachelor of Arts in Biology from Franklin & Marshall College in 1976. A foundation in biological science provided preparation for the more specialized study that followed. Dentistry depends on a detailed understanding of anatomy, physiology, pathology and the relationships between oral and systemic health.

After his undergraduate education, Dr. Dunn pursued graduate study at Roswell Park Memorial Institute. His professional profile states that he earned a Master of Science in Head and Neck Cancer in 1977. This period of study added a research-oriented dimension to his education before dental school.

Dr. Dunn earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the New York University College of Dentistry in 1980. Dental school provided the clinical and scientific training required to enter the profession. His subsequent career would combine general dental practice with specialized continuing education and professional leadership.

Following dental school, Dr. Dunn served as a Commissioned Officer in the United States Public Health Service from 1980 to 1982, according to his professional biography. Public health service gave him an early professional setting outside conventional private practice. It also added a service-oriented dimension to a career that later included leadership in local and regional dental organizations.

A 2020 professional profile described Dr. Dunn as having worked in dentistry since 1982 and as practicing in Elmira since 1988. Over a career of this length, dentistry has changed substantially. Digital imaging, implant technology, restorative materials, computerized systems and the growing field of dental sleep medicine have all expanded the tools available to practitioners. Dr. Dunn's record shows sustained involvement with continuing professional development as these changes occurred.

Dr. Dunn became closely associated with Chemung Family Dental in Elmira. His professional profile described him as the practice's owner. The practice model emphasized comprehensive care and individualized treatment planning. Current directory records continue to associate Richard B. Dunn, DDS, with dentistry at 1007 Broadway in Elmira, though specific ownership and current administrative roles should be verified before being described as current.

General dentistry encompasses prevention, diagnosis and treatment across a broad range of oral-health needs. Dr. Dunn's professional history includes restorative and general dental care as well as interests in implant dentistry and sleep-related oral appliance therapy. A broad clinical foundation can be especially important for dentists who later pursue additional areas of advanced training.

One of the most distinctive areas of Dr. Dunn's professional work has been dental sleep medicine. This field focuses on the role dentists can play in evaluating and managing certain sleep-related breathing problems in collaboration with physicians and sleep specialists. For appropriately diagnosed patients, dentists with relevant training may provide oral appliance therapy for conditions such as obstructive sleep apnea.

Dr. Dunn's professional biography identifies him as a Diplomate of the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine (ABDSM). The credential reflects advanced professional involvement in dental sleep medicine. His biography also reported service as a director of the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine. Because board and organizational positions can change over time, the director role should be described historically unless independently verified as current.

Dr. Dunn also completed a year-long fellowship involving medical competency for the treatment of temporomandibular disorders and sleep dentistry, according to his 2020 professional profile. Temporomandibular disorders involve the jaw joint and surrounding structures. Their evaluation can be complex because symptoms may overlap with dental, muscular and other medical conditions.

Dr. Dunn's professional biography states that he has lectured locally and nationally on dental sleep medicine. Professional lectures allow clinicians to share experience and research with colleagues. They also contribute to continuing education in fields where diagnostic and treatment standards continue to evolve.

Dr. Dunn has also participated in published scientific discussion. PubMed independently records Richard B. Dunn as a co-author, with Mickey C. Harrison and Jody Kear, of a 2018 letter in the Annals of the American Thoracic Society. The publication, "Comments on 'Stressing the Brain...Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome'," provides independent confirmation of his participation in peer-reviewed medical literature.

The connection between dentistry and sleep medicine illustrates how health disciplines increasingly overlap. Oral structures and jaw position can influence the upper airway. Dentists working in sleep medicine therefore operate at an intersection involving oral health, airway anatomy and physician-diagnosed sleep disorders. Appropriate collaboration is essential because diagnosis of sleep apnea and management of broader medical risks require qualified medical professionals.

Dr. Dunn's professional biography identifies him as a Fellow of the American College of Dentists. Fellowship in professional organizations can reflect peer recognition and professional service. The FACD designation appears alongside his name in subsequent professional profiles.

His professional affiliations have included the American Dental Association, New York State Dental Association, American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine, American Thoracic Society, Academy of General Dentistry, Academy of Osseointegration, Academy of Digital and Computerized Dentistry and Pierre Fauchard Academy, according to his 2020 profile. Memberships should be understood as historically reported unless current status is separately verified. Together, they demonstrate the range of Dr. Dunn's professional interests.

Dr. Dunn has held leadership positions within the Sixth District Dental Society of the State of New York. His professional profile identifies him as a former president of the organization and, as of 2020, chairman of its Malpractice Claims Committee. Professional society leadership allows dentists to participate in issues extending beyond their individual practices, including professional standards, education and organized dentistry.

Dr. Dunn also served as president of the Chemung County Dental Society, according to his professional biography. Local dental organizations help connect practitioners within a community and provide opportunities for education, collaboration and professional service. This leadership complements his clinical work in Elmira.

His professional history also includes service as director of the Southern Tier Implant Study Club. Implant dentistry requires understanding of treatment planning, restorative dentistry, surgical considerations and long-term maintenance. Study clubs allow clinicians to discuss cases, technologies and evolving professional approaches.

The breadth of Dr. Dunn's professional affiliations and advanced training reflects an emphasis on continuing education. Dentistry is a technology-intensive profession. Materials, digital systems, imaging, implant techniques and sleep-related therapies continue to evolve. Long careers therefore require practitioners to continually update their knowledge.

In November 2018, Continental Who's Who announced recognition of Richard B. Dunn, DDS, as a Pinnacle Lifetime Member in the field of dentistry. The recognition highlighted his career and professional contributions. As with other directory-based honors, the designation should be attributed specifically to Continental Who's Who and should not be presented as an independent clinical-quality ranking.

Independent current directories continue to identify Richard B. Dunn, DDS, as a general-practice dentist in Elmira. The American Dental Association's Find-a-Dentist directory lists him in general practice at 1007 Broadway Street. This provides current corroboration of his professional identity and Elmira association.

Dr. Dunn's professional record extends beyond direct patient treatment. He has served professional societies. He has participated in dental education. He has lectured on dental sleep medicine. He has contributed to scientific literature. He has been involved in implant-focused professional education. These activities illustrate how an established dental career can combine clinical practice with leadership and scholarship.

Dental sleep medicine is particularly dependent on collaboration. Dentists can contribute specialized oral-appliance expertise, but sleep-disordered breathing can carry significant medical implications. Responsible care therefore requires communication among dentists, physicians and sleep professionals. Dr. Dunn's involvement in both dental and thoracic/sleep-medicine professional communities reflects this interdisciplinary environment.

From biological science and head-and-neck study to dental school, public health service, private dentistry and sleep medicine, Dr. Dunn's career has developed across multiple areas of health care. His professional leadership has extended that influence into dental societies, education and organized dentistry.

Dynamo Times recognizes Richard B. Dunn, DDS, FACD, for a longstanding career in dentistry, dental sleep medicine, professional education and leadership.

About Chemung Family Dental

Richard B. Dunn, DDS, FACD, is a dentist associated with Elmira, New York. He earned a BA in Biology from Franklin & Marshall College in 1976, an MS in Head and Neck Cancer from Roswell Park Memorial Institute in 1977 and a DDS from the New York University College of Dentistry in 1980. His professional biography documents service as a commissioned officer in the U.S. Public Health Service, decades in dentistry, leadership in professional dental organizations and advanced involvement in dental sleep medicine. He is identified professionally as a Fellow of the American College of Dentists and a Diplomate of the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine.
