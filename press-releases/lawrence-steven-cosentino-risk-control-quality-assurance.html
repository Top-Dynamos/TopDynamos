Lawrence Steven Cosentino Brings Naval Nuclear Experience, Technical Expertise and Continuous Improvement to Risk Control

Risk control consultant and U.S. Navy veteran Lawrence Steven Cosentino has built a career that runs from nuclear electrical systems and emergency response to loss control, quality assurance and insurance risk evaluation.

TravelersOssining, NYAugust 28, 2026

OSSINING, N.Y. Long before he walked client sites for an insurer, Lawrence Steven Cosentino kept the electrical systems running aboard nuclear submarines. That training in procedure and safety still shapes how he works today as a risk control consultant, in a career that has moved through technical systems, quality assurance, inspection and insurance risk management.

Cosentino has served as a risk control consultant with Travelers since 2022. Through fieldwork, he evaluates client properties and equipment for potential liabilities and insurance risks, then reports findings that support informed decisions. The job asks him to catch problems before they grow into larger losses and to turn technical observations into information that organizations and insurers can use.

His path began in the United States Navy, where he served from 2006 to 2014 as a nuclear electrician. He worked with steam powered and electrical systems aboard submarines including the USS Jacksonville and later the USS Missouri. Nuclear propulsion leaves no room for loose procedure, and Cosentino has credited the Navy with teaching him an open minded approach to decisions rather than acting from habit. That outlook proved valuable in a field where conditions change and an established practice is not always the safest one.

Leaving the service meant translating specialized skills into civilian work. Cosentino became a senior technician on an emergency response team at GlobalFoundries and later a maintenance supervisor at Gourmet Boutique. Both roles widened his experience with operational reliability and workplace risk in industrial settings.

He then moved into loss control with Hartford Steam Boiler Inspection and Insurance Company, doing technical and engineering related work. Loss control examines conditions that could lead to equipment failure, property damage, injury or other insured losses. Its purpose is preventive: find the weak point early and give the organization a chance to fix it. With his background in complex equipment, the field was a natural next step.

At Hartford Steam Boiler, Cosentino received an Award for Exemplary Integrity in 2019. Integrity carries real weight in inspection work, since clients, insurers and regulators rely on the accuracy of what a professional records. A consultant has to document conditions objectively and state concerns plainly, even when the findings are unwelcome.

Risk control also means asking whether existing procedures still make sense. Cosentino's review of outdated practices has been credited with helping avoid substantial city mandated inspection expenses. He holds Lean Six Sigma Green Belt and Black Belt certifications from Villanova University, methods built on cutting waste and variation, measuring results and deciding with data. A recurring finding may point to a weak process, and an unnecessary step may cost time without adding safety.

Cosentino earned a Bachelor of Science in Computer Information Systems from Manhattan College, now Manhattan University, in 2005. He has also been pursuing a Master of Business Administration with a concentration in Business Analytics. Analytics gives field experience a second lens, helping a risk professional spot patterns, measure problems and judge performance.

Quality assurance runs through every stage of his career. Strict standards governed his Navy work around nuclear propulsion, careful procedures kept industrial equipment reliable, and inspections in loss control exposed hazards before they caused harm. In risk consulting the same discipline applies to client properties and equipment. Success in the field is often measured by the accident that never happens.

His sense of service extends beyond work. In 2022, Village Lutheran Church in Bronxville, New York, commissioned him as a deacon. He has taken part in environmental volunteer work and is involved with VFW Post 2285, American Legion Post 979 and the Eastchester Veterans Foundation, continuing a commitment that began in the Navy.

Dynamo Times recognizes Lawrence Steven Cosentino for his contributions to quality assurance, risk control, operational improvement, technical leadership and community service.

About Travelers

Lawrence Steven Cosentino is a risk control consultant at Travelers and a United States Navy veteran based in Ossining, New York. He served as a nuclear electrician aboard submarines including the USS Jacksonville and USS Missouri from 2006 to 2014, then worked in loss control at Hartford Steam Boiler Inspection and Insurance Company and in technical roles at GlobalFoundries and Gourmet Boutique. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer Information Systems from Manhattan College and Lean Six Sigma Green Belt and Black Belt certifications from Villanova University.
