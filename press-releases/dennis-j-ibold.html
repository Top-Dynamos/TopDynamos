Dennis J. Ibold Reflects More Than Five Decades of Legal Advocacy, Leadership and Client Service

Ibold & O'Brien founding attorney Dennis J. Ibold has built a legal career spanning more than 50 years, with experience across family law, personal injury, real estate, business law and litigation.

Ibold & O'BrienChardon, OHJuly 22, 2026

CHARDON, Ohio: The Dynamo Times highlights Dennis J. Ibold for a distinguished legal career spanning more than five decades and characterized by client advocacy, professional leadership and a longstanding commitment to the communities of Northeast Ohio.

Mr. Ibold is a founding attorney of Ibold & O'Brien, a full-service Ohio law firm whose history dates to 1980. Throughout his career, Mr. Ibold has developed substantial experience across several areas of law, with his practice emphasizing family law, personal injury, real estate, business law and litigation. For Mr. Ibold, one principle has remained particularly important throughout those years: conscientious, responsible work on behalf of his clients.

Mr. Ibold was admitted to practice law in Ohio in 1973 and subsequently gained admission to the U.S. Federal Courts in 1975. Over the five decades that followed, he developed a broad legal practice while establishing deep roots within Northeast Ohio. Laws have evolved, technology has changed how attorneys research and manage cases, and client expectations have shifted, yet the fundamental responsibility of an attorney remains largely unchanged: understanding a client's circumstances, applying the law and advocating responsibly for that client's interests.

A defining chapter of Mr. Ibold's professional career began in 1980 when he co-founded the firm that ultimately became Ibold & O'Brien. Over the decades, the practice developed into a significant full-service law firm serving clients throughout Northeast Ohio, with offices in Chardon and Orwell. Building a law firm across multiple decades requires more than legal knowledge: it requires leadership, maintaining professional standards, developing attorneys and staff, and earning the trust of clients and communities over time.

Family law has been a particularly significant area of Mr. Ibold's professional practice. Family-law matters can involve some of the most personal and emotionally difficult circumstances clients encounter: divorce, child custody, visitation, property division, spousal support and child support. These cases involve legal questions, but they also involve families undergoing significant transitions. Effective representation therefore requires more than knowledge of statutes and court procedures: an attorney must be able to evaluate complex circumstances while recognizing the personal impact legal decisions can have on clients and their families.

Mr. Ibold is a member of the American Academy of Matrimonial Lawyers. Membership in the organization reflects substantial experience and professional standing within family and matrimonial law. The Academy brings together attorneys whose practices focus significantly on matters involving marriage, divorce, custody, property division and related family-law issues. For Mr. Ibold, involvement with the organization complements decades of direct experience representing clients in family-law matters.

Although family law represents an important component of his career, Mr. Ibold's practice extends considerably beyond matrimonial matters, encompassing personal injury, real estate, business law and litigation. This range has allowed him to represent clients facing very different kinds of legal challenges: a family navigating divorce, an injured person requiring representation following an accident, a property owner encountering a real estate dispute, or a business needing counsel regarding a commercial matter.

Personal injury law represents another important part of Mr. Ibold's practice. Individuals seeking representation following an injury may be facing medical treatment, insurance issues, financial concerns and uncertainty about what comes next. Decades of legal practice provide attorneys with perspective developed through exposure to many different cases and circumstances, experience that can be particularly valuable when determining how to approach complex disputes.

Mr. Ibold's path toward law began with his undergraduate education at Marquette University, where he earned his Bachelor of Arts degree in 1970. He subsequently attended Cleveland State University College of Law, earning his Juris Doctor in 1973, the same year he was admitted to the Ohio Bar. His education provided the foundation for a professional career that would ultimately extend for more than half a century.

Mr. Ibold's career has received extensive recognition within the legal community. He has been selected as an Ohio Super Lawyer for numerous consecutive years and recognized among the Top 100 family law attorneys in Ohio. In 2018, the American Society of Legal Advocates recognized him among its Top 100 Family Law Lawyers in Ohio. Mr. Ibold has also received a BV Distinguished Rating through the Martindale-Hubbell Bar Register of Preeminent Attorneys.

More than 50 years in legal practice creates a perspective that cannot be replicated quickly. An experienced attorney has witnessed changing laws, different judges, different economic environments, changes in family structures, changes in business, changes in technology, and countless individual client circumstances. While every case remains unique, experience can help an attorney identify issues, anticipate complications and evaluate possible strategies.

Ibold & O'Brien emphasizes providing clients with honest, innovative and high-quality legal counsel. For clients, legal problems can be intimidating: the terminology may be unfamiliar, court procedures may be confusing, and the potential consequences can be significant. One of an attorney's responsibilities is therefore helping clients understand the situation they are facing. Mr. Ibold's longstanding approach emphasizes responsibility to the client and the importance of conscientious work.

A professional legacy in law is not measured solely through cases won or professional recognitions. It can also be found in the clients assisted over decades: the families guided through difficult transitions, the businesses advised, the disputes resolved, the attorneys mentored, and the firm built to continue serving its community. Mr. Ibold's role in establishing and developing Ibold & O'Brien represents an important part of that legacy. More than four decades after the firm's founding, it continues providing legal services across Northeast Ohio.

The Dynamo Times recognizes Dennis J. Ibold for his longstanding contributions to the legal profession and for a career characterized by advocacy, leadership, experience and dedication to client service.

About Ibold & O'Brien

Dennis J. Ibold is a founding attorney of Ibold & O'Brien in Chardon, Ohio. He has more than 50 years of legal experience and focuses his practice on family law, personal injury, real estate, business law and litigation. Mr. Ibold earned his Bachelor of Arts from Marquette University in 1970 and his Juris Doctor from Cleveland State University College of Law in 1973. He was admitted to the Ohio Bar in 1973 and to the U.S. Federal Courts in 1975. His professional affiliations include the Geauga County Bar Association, Ohio State Bar Association and American Academy of Matrimonial Lawyers. Over the course of his career, Mr. Ibold has received numerous professional distinctions recognizing his work in family and matrimonial law.
