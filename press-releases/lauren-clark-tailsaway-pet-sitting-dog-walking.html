Lauren Clark Builds Tailsaway Around Personalized Pet Care, Safety and the Comforts of Home

Natick pet-care professional Lauren Clark has operated Tailsaway Pet Sitting and Dog Walking since 2001, building a local business around individualized care for dogs and cats through in-home visits, small-group outings and overnight care.

Tailsaway Pet Sitting and Dog WalkingNatick, MASeptember 22, 2026

Dynamo Times highlights Lauren Clark, owner of Tailsaway Pet Sitting and Dog Walking, a Natick-based pet-care business serving dogs, cats and their families through personalized walking, sitting, boarding and in-home care.

Public business records indicate that Tailsaway has been operating since 2001, giving Clark more than two decades of experience building relationships with pets and the people who care for them. Her PetWorks profile emphasizes a high-touch model focused particularly on small dogs and cats, with services designed around individual routines rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.

Pet care depends heavily on trust. Owners are not simply purchasing a walk or an overnight stay. They are placing an animal they consider part of the family in someone else's care. The Better Business Bureau independently lists the business as having started on November 18, 2001, confirming a professional history extending more than two decades.

Tailsaway operates from Natick, Massachusetts, serving clients in the surrounding MetroWest area. BBB records identify Lauren Clark as the owner and principal contact and classify the company as a sole proprietorship. The business is listed across categories including dog walking, pet sitting, boarding and pet exercise. Its longevity reflects the relationship-based nature of local service businesses, where repeat clients and reputation can become central to growth.

Clark's PetWorks profile places particular emphasis on small dogs. Rather than creating very large groups, she describes limiting pack sizes to approximately three to five dogs to provide more individualized attention. Small-group care can allow a provider to pay closer attention to personalities, routines and interactions among dogs. The goal is to create an engaging experience while maintaining a manageable environment.

Tailsaway's services include dog walking as well as outings to local parks and other activities. For many dogs, a walk provides more than physical exercise. It can offer stimulation, environmental enrichment and an opportunity to maintain a routine while an owner is working or traveling. Clark's profile describes these outings as an important part of the personalized experience she creates for her canine clients.

Tailsaway also provides in-home pet sitting. For some animals, remaining in familiar surroundings can help preserve established routines while their owners are away. The PetWorks listing describes scheduled visits that may include feeding, walking and playtime. Clark's services can also include practical household tasks such as bringing in mail, watering plants and light housekeeping.

The business offers overnight pet care as well. The PetWorks profile describes in-home overnight stays that allow animals to remain in their familiar environment rather than requiring a change of setting. Overnight arrangements can be especially useful for owners whose pets need companionship, regular routines or more continuous supervision. Specific services and suitability should always be discussed directly with the provider before booking.

Tailsaway's work is not limited to dogs. Clark also provides care for cats, including in-home visits while owners are away. Cat care can involve feeding, litter maintenance, companionship and monitoring for changes in behavior. Her profile also notes household support such as collecting mail and watering plants during pet-sitting visits.

PetWorks lists pet medication administration among Tailsaway's services. Medication routines can be an important consideration when selecting a sitter for an animal with ongoing health needs. Pet owners should provide complete instructions and appropriate veterinary contact information and should confirm that the requested medication task is within the provider's training and permitted scope.

Clark's PetWorks profile lists her as American Red Cross Pet First Aid and CPR Certified. Emergency-preparedness education can be valuable for pet-care professionals because animals may experience injuries or sudden illness while their owners are away. First-aid preparation does not replace veterinary care. Instead, it can help a caregiver recognize an emergency and respond appropriately while professional veterinary assistance is being obtained.

Clark is also listed as a Certified Animal Reiki Practitioner and Certified Animal Reiki Teacher. Her profile describes Reiki as an optional wellness-oriented service intended to support relaxation. It is important to distinguish such complementary wellness services from veterinary medicine. Reiki should not be presented as a diagnosis, treatment or cure for disease and should never replace appropriate veterinary evaluation or treatment.

PetWorks identifies Tailsaway as offering mobile services. A mobile model can make pet care more convenient by bringing services to the animal's home. For owners, this can reduce transportation requirements and help maintain familiar routines. For pets, it allows much of their care to occur in the environment they already know.

In-home pet sitting often carries a second responsibility: caring for the household while the owner is away. Tailsaway's listing includes mail collection, plant watering and light housekeeping among the tasks that may accompany pet care. These details can make a difference for clients taking extended trips and illustrate how professional pet sitting can overlap with practical home-care responsibilities.

The Better Business Bureau lists Tailsaway Pet Sitting and Dog Walking as an accredited business with an A+ rating at the time this profile was prepared. BBB records state that the company has been accredited since March 31, 2021. BBB also identifies Clark as the owner and reports two employees. A BBB rating should be understood as the bureau's rating under its own methodology rather than as an independent guarantee of future service quality.

Tailsaway's public presence reflects a small-business model built around direct relationships. Pet owners frequently want consistency, knowing who will enter their home, who will handle their pet and how that person will communicate if something unexpected happens. For a small pet-care company, personal familiarity can become part of the service itself. Clark's business emphasizes individualized attention rather than a large institutional model.

Animals can become accustomed to predictable schedules. Meals, walks, sleep, play and medication may all occur at familiar times. A pet sitter's role often involves maintaining as much of that routine as practical while the owner is away. Tailsaway's emphasis on customized home visits reflects this approach.

Running a pet-care company requires more than affection for animals. It also involves scheduling, client communication, safety procedures, home access, transportation, service policies and the responsibility of operating inside clients' homes. Clark has turned her interest in animals into a long-running local enterprise. The business's history since 2001 demonstrates the operational side of pet-care entrepreneurship.

Public trademark records show that the TAILSAWAY word mark was federally registered in March 2024 for dog walking and pet-sitting services. Trademark registration can help a small business protect the identity it has built over time. For Tailsaway, the registration formalizes a name associated with more than two decades of pet-care activity.

Although the service centers on animals, professional pet sitting also serves people. Owners need confidence that their pets are safe while they work, travel or manage other responsibilities. Communication and reliability can therefore matter almost as much as the walk, feeding or overnight stay itself. A successful pet-care relationship requires trust on both sides.

More than two decades after beginning in pet care, Clark continues to provide services for pet owners in Natick and surrounding Massachusetts communities. Her business combines traditional pet sitting and dog walking with small-group outings, overnight care, medication support and optional animal-wellness services.

Dynamo Times recognizes Lauren Clark and Tailsaway Pet Sitting and Dog Walking for building a long-running local business centered on individualized pet care, safety, reliability and personal attention.

About Tailsaway Pet Sitting and Dog Walking

Lauren Clark is the owner of Tailsaway Pet Sitting and Dog Walking, a Natick, Massachusetts pet-care business. PetWorks lists her services as including in-home pet sitting, pet medication administration, boarding, dog walking, overnight care and cat care. Her listed certifications include American Red Cross Pet First Aid and CPR, Certified Animal Reiki Practitioner and Certified Animal Reiki Teacher. BBB records identify Tailsaway as a business operating since 2001 and Lauren Clark as its owner.
