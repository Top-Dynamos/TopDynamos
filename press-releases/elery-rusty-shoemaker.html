Elery M. "Rusty" Shoemaker, EdD, PMP, DTM, Reflects a 45-Year Commitment to Nuclear Power, Safety and Leadership

Nuclear power professional Elery M. "Rusty" Shoemaker has built a decades-long career encompassing nuclear operations, organizational leadership, peer review, training and the development of industry professionals.

Institute of Nuclear Power Operations / WANOAcworth, GAJuly 21, 2026

ACWORTH, Ga.: The Dynamo Times highlights Elery M. "Rusty" Shoemaker, EdD, PMP, DTM, for a professional career spanning approximately 45 years in the nuclear power industry and distinguished by leadership, education, training and a commitment to safe and reliable nuclear operations.

Dr. Shoemaker has spent decades working within one of the world's most technically demanding industries. Since 1995, his career has included multiple positions with the Institute of Nuclear Power Operations (INPO) and the World Association of Nuclear Operators (WANO).

Among his responsibilities, Dr. Shoemaker has served as a senior corporate team leader and has led corporate peer review teams. His work has placed him in positions focused not simply on technical performance, but also on leadership development and organizational effectiveness within the nuclear power industry.

His professional philosophy has emphasized the importance of developing people alongside systems and processes. Throughout his work with INPO and WANO, Dr. Shoemaker has contributed to efforts focused on leadership and the safe and reliable operation of nuclear power facilities.

Before his work with INPO and WANO, Dr. Shoemaker spent approximately 15 years with Carolina Power & Light, an organization that later became part of Progress Energy and Duke Energy. From 1980 through 1995, he served in various roles that helped establish the foundation for his expertise in nuclear operations.

In 1983, Dr. Shoemaker earned credentials as a Senior Reactor Operator for a Westinghouse pressurized water reactor, a qualification he maintained through 1995. The experience provided an important foundation for a career that would eventually expand from plant operations into training, accreditation, peer review and organizational leadership.

Dr. Shoemaker complemented his extensive technical experience with a significant academic background. He earned a Bachelor of Arts in physics from Thomas More University in 1979, followed by a Bachelor of Science in mechanical engineering from the University of Kentucky in 1980.

As his career evolved toward organizational and leadership responsibilities, he continued his education at Nova Southeastern University, earning a Master of Science in organizational leadership in 2012 and later completing a Doctor of Education in organizational leadership in 2022.

The combination of physics, mechanical engineering and organizational leadership has given Dr. Shoemaker a multidisciplinary perspective encompassing both the technical and human sides of complex organizations.

Continuous professional development has remained an important component of his career. He earned the Project Management Professional (PMP) credential from the Project Management Institute in 2005. His additional professional training has included certifications through the National Academy for Nuclear Training, including INPO Event Investigation, INPO Facilitator and INPO Instructor designations.

He also achieved the designation of Distinguished Toastmaster (DTM) through Toastmasters International in 2013. Together, these credentials reflect expertise extending across engineering, nuclear operations, project management, training, communication and leadership.

One of the defining themes of Dr. Shoemaker's career has been the development of other professionals. His work has included responsibilities related to peer reviews and organizational performance: areas where technical knowledge must be combined with communication, judgment and the ability to work effectively with leadership teams.

In highly regulated and technically complex environments such as nuclear power, organizational culture and leadership can be critical components of performance. Dr. Shoemaker's professional journey demonstrates how technical expertise can evolve into broader organizational influence.

Over the course of his career, Dr. Shoemaker has received several forms of professional recognition. His honors have included the INPO World Class Award, as well as multiple INPO Above & Beyond and Team Excellence awards. In 2022, he also received recognition as a Kentucky Colonel, an honorary appointment bestowed by the Governor of Kentucky.

His professional affiliations have included organizations such as the American Nuclear Society, the Project Management Institute, Golden Key International Honor Society and Delta Epsilon Iota Academic Honor Society.

Dr. Shoemaker's commitment to leadership and service extends beyond his professional responsibilities. His community involvement has included religious service as a deacon and Sunday school teacher as well as support for charitable organizations including the Atlanta Community Food Bank and The Salvation Army.

After approximately four and a half decades in nuclear power, Dr. Shoemaker's professional story represents an unusual combination of operational experience, engineering knowledge, education and organizational leadership. His career demonstrates that maintaining excellence in a technically sophisticated industry requires more than technology: it also requires knowledgeable professionals, strong leadership, continuous learning and a commitment to developing the next generation.

The Dynamo Times recognizes Elery M. "Rusty" Shoemaker, EdD, PMP, DTM, for his longstanding contributions to nuclear power and for a professional journey characterized by technical expertise, leadership development, education and service.

About Institute of Nuclear Power Operations / WANO

Elery M. "Rusty" Shoemaker, EdD, PMP, DTM, is a nuclear power professional with approximately 45 years of industry experience. His career has included leadership responsibilities with the Institute of Nuclear Power Operations (INPO) and the World Association of Nuclear Operators (WANO), as well as earlier nuclear operations experience with Carolina Power & Light. His professional background encompasses nuclear operations, corporate peer reviews, organizational leadership, training, project management and leadership development. Dr. Shoemaker holds degrees in physics and mechanical engineering as well as graduate degrees in organizational leadership.
