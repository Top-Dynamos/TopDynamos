Merle S. Robboy, MD, Builds a More Than 50-Year Legacy in Obstetrics, Gynecology and Women's Health

Board-certified obstetrician-gynecologist Merle S. Robboy, MD, built a career spanning private practice, hospital medicine, university teaching, family planning and public service in Southern California.

Private PracticeSan Diego, CASeptember 22, 2026

Dynamo Times highlights Merle S. Robboy, MD, a board-certified obstetrician-gynecologist whose professional biography documents more than five decades of work in women's health, private practice, hospital medicine, medical education and family-planning services.

In 2018, Marquis Who's Who presented Dr. Robboy with its Albert Nelson Marquis Lifetime Achievement Award, following an earlier 2017 profile recognizing his longstanding work in obstetrics and gynecology. His career reflects the breadth of the specialty itself, combining medicine, surgery, reproductive health, education and direct patient care.

Dr. Robboy's path into medicine began with undergraduate study at Case Western Reserve University, where he earned a Bachelor of Arts in 1961. He subsequently attended The Ohio State University College of Medicine, earning his Doctor of Medicine in 1965. His professional biography states that he was drawn to obstetrics and gynecology because the specialty combines medical care with surgical skills. That combination would shape the decades that followed.

After medical school, Dr. Robboy began his postgraduate career as a rotating intern at a U.S. Public Health Service Hospital from 1965 to 1966. He then continued service with the U.S. Public Health Service from 1966 through 1968. His professional profiles state that he rose to the rank of Lieutenant Commander before receiving an honorable discharge. This early period added public service to the clinical foundation of his career.

From 1968 to 1972, Dr. Robboy completed residency training at the UCLA Center for Health Sciences. Residency in obstetrics and gynecology requires physicians to develop expertise across women's health, pregnancy, reproductive medicine and surgery. The training prepared him for a career that would include both office-based gynecology and hospital care.

Dr. Robboy's professional biography also documents residency experience at City of Hope in 1972 involving general and gynecologic surgery. His earlier Marquis profile described this work in connection with surgery-oncology. This additional training expanded the surgical component of his medical background.

In 1972, Dr. Robboy established Merle S. Robboy, MD, A Professional Corporation. The 2018 Marquis profile reported that he had worked as a gynecologist in that private practice since 1972. Maintaining a medical practice over multiple decades requires adaptation to changes in technology, clinical standards, insurance systems and patient expectations. His professional history therefore spans several distinct eras of American medicine.

Dr. Robboy also maintained a long association with Hoag Memorial Hospital Presbyterian. The 2018 profile reported 46 years on active staff at the hospital. Long-term hospital affiliation can place a physician within generations of changes in clinical practice and institutional medicine. For an obstetrician-gynecologist, it also creates continuity between outpatient care and hospital-based procedures.

Medical education became another major component of Dr. Robboy's career. His Marquis profile states that he served as a clinical associate professor in the Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology at the University of California, Irvine from 1972 to 2003. His professional profile also describes teaching resident physicians and medical students in clinical and surgical settings. Academic clinical appointments allow experienced physicians to pass practical knowledge to future generations of doctors.

Dr. Robboy's career also included substantial work in family-planning medicine. The 2018 profile states that he worked as a contract physician for the Planned Parenthood Federation of America from 1997 to 2012 and served in an interim leadership role involving family-planning services. An earlier 2017 profile described approximately 15 years of contract physician and interim-director service. Family-planning medicine can involve contraception, reproductive-health counseling and other clinical services requiring specialized medical judgment and patient communication.

From 2003 to 2014, Dr. Robboy worked as a contract physician for Kaiser Permanente Hospital, according to the 2018 profile. This role added another major health-care system to his professional experience. His career therefore included private practice, academic medicine, hospital affiliations, public-health service and large-system clinical work.

The 2018 biography also reported a courtesy-staff relationship with Fountain Valley Regional Hospital and Medical Center beginning in 2003. His hospital affiliations over the course of his career illustrate the breadth of his work within Southern California medicine.

An earlier Marquis profile described Dr. Robboy's principal specialties as obstetrics, gynecology and infertility. These fields encompass care across many stages of women's reproductive health. His professional biography notes that delivering babies was among the most fulfilling parts of his career. That aspect of medicine creates a distinctive physician-patient relationship, combining clinical responsibility with some of the most consequential moments in a family's life.

Dr. Robboy was certified by the American Board of Obstetrics and Gynecology, according to the source profile. Board certification represents specialized postgraduate training and successful completion of the certification requirements established by the relevant medical specialty board. His biography also identifies him as a Fellow of the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists.

Dr. Robboy's involvement in medicine extended beyond his own practice. He held multiple leadership positions with the Orange County Obstetrical and Gynecological Society. His 2018 profile lists service as treasurer from 1980 to 1981, secretary from 1981 to 1982, vice president from 1982 to 1983 and president from 1983 to 1984. Progression through those positions reflects sustained participation in the professional community surrounding his specialty.

His professional affiliations included organizations associated with fertility, gynecologic surgery and obstetrics and gynecology. The Marquis profiles identify involvement with the American Fertility Society, American Association of Gynecologic Laparoscopists and Pacific Coast Obstetrical and Gynecological Society. Professional societies give physicians opportunities to exchange clinical knowledge, follow developments in their specialties and contribute to professional education.

Dr. Robboy also contributed to medical literature. His 2018 profile states that he authored articles appearing in publications including the American Journal of Clinical Nutrition and Obstetrics & Gynecology. Scientific and clinical writing allows physicians to share observations and research with a broader professional audience. It also extends a clinician's contribution beyond the patients seen directly in practice.

In addition to paid professional work, Dr. Robboy's biography documents volunteer gynecological service with the SOS Clinic, beginning in 1976. Volunteer medical work can expand access to care for people who may otherwise face barriers to obtaining services. This aspect of his career adds community service to a professional history already spanning private practice, hospitals, universities and public service.

A physician practicing for more than 50 years witnesses extraordinary changes. Diagnostic technology advances. Surgical techniques evolve. Medications change. Health systems consolidate. Patient expectations and standards of informed decision-making develop. Dr. Robboy's professional record stretches across these transformations. His career therefore represents not only longevity but adaptation.

In August 2018, Marquis Who's Who announced that Dr. Robboy had received the Albert Nelson Marquis Lifetime Achievement Award. Marquis described the recognition as honoring his experience, professional accomplishments, leadership and contributions within medicine. The award should be understood specifically as recognition conferred by Marquis Who's Who rather than as an independent medical-quality rating.

The lifetime-achievement announcement followed a December 2017 Marquis profile recognizing Dr. Robboy for his dedication to obstetrics and gynecology. That earlier profile emphasized his more than four decades of private medical practice, work in infertility and reproductive medicine, hospital affiliations, medical education and professional memberships. Together, the two profiles provide a detailed historical record of his career through that period.

Medicine was not Dr. Robboy's only longstanding interest. His professional biographies describe extensive involvement with orchids. He was a lifetime member of the Newport Harbor Orchid Society and participated in the American Orchid Society. He also lectured and educated others on orchids and contributed to local orchid organizations.

Dr. Robboy's work in the orchid community earned recognition outside medicine. His professional profile lists a Diamond Award from Orchid Digest in 2015 for contributions to the Newport Harbor Orchid Society. The honor illustrates another side of a career often defined publicly by medicine. Expertise, education and community participation also characterized his horticultural interests.

His interests extended to dahlias as well. The Marquis profile identifies Dr. Robboy as a judge with the American Dahlia Society. Becoming a flower-show judge requires specialized knowledge very different from medical training. The combination illustrates the range of interests he pursued alongside a demanding medical career.

From medical school in the 1960s through decades of private practice and hospital medicine, Dr. Robboy built a professional record encompassing obstetrics, gynecology, infertility, family planning, teaching, public service and professional leadership. His work touched patients directly while also contributing to the education of physicians and the activities of medical organizations.

Dynamo Times recognizes Merle S. Robboy, MD, for a career of more than 50 years devoted to women's health, medical education, public service and professional engagement.

About Private Practice

Merle S. Robboy, MD, is a board-certified obstetrician-gynecologist whose professional biography documents more than five decades in medicine. He earned a BA from Case Western Reserve University in 1961 and an MD from The Ohio State University College of Medicine in 1965. His career included service in the U.S. Public Health Service, residency at UCLA, surgical training at City of Hope, private practice beginning in 1972, a long clinical association with Hoag Memorial Hospital Presbyterian, teaching at the University of California, Irvine, family-planning work and contract physician service with Kaiser Permanente. In 2018, Marquis Who's Who presented Dr. Robboy with its Albert Nelson Marquis Lifetime Achievement Award.
