Amy Salanty Built Purple Elephant Psychiatry & Wellness Around Care That Fits Real Lives

Her fully virtual practice sees adolescents, adults and older adults across Florida, offers early, late and Saturday appointments, and draws on 28 years of nursing and a military family's understanding of service life.

Purple Elephant Psychiatry & WellnessHollywood, FLNovember 20, 2026

HOLLYWOOD, Fla. "I believe seeking help is a courageous step," Amy Salanty tells the people who find her practice. Making that step easier is the reason she opened Purple Elephant Psychiatry & Wellness in July 2026.

Anyone in Florida can be seen there, because every visit happens online. Visits take place on a secure, HIPAA compliant telehealth platform. Patients receive a link before each session and can join from a phone, tablet or computer, without a drive or a waiting room.

Ms. Salanty built the schedule around real lives. Appointments are available before and after standard business hours and on Saturdays, so a parent, a shift worker or a student does not have to choose between work and care.

She sees adolescents, adults and older adults. Her focus areas include anxiety, ADHD, depression, sleep disorders and trauma, and she treats a wide range of other mental health conditions.

Her approach starts with listening. A first visit is a full evaluation of symptoms, history and goals, and the plan is built together from there. Medication is one option, not a requirement. Therapy, lifestyle support and collaborative planning are all part of the conversation.

Service life is close to home for her. Her husband, Dave, served 21 years in the Navy, and as part of a military family she understands the particular strain that service members, spouses and veterans carry.

She brings 28 years of nursing to the work. Ms. Salanty is a board certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner, with a master's degree from Wilkes University, and she spent most of her career in intensive care before turning to mental health.

Her plans for the practice keep growing. Ketamine and Spravato treatment are on her list for the future, and so is more schooling focused on older adults, whose physical and emotional health she wants to care for side by side.

"My goal is to meet you where you are," she says. Dynamo Times recognizes Amy Salanty for building a practice that does exactly that, for families across Florida.

About Purple Elephant Psychiatry & Wellness

Amy Salanty, MSN, RN, is a board certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner in Hollywood, Florida, and the founder of Purple Elephant Psychiatry & Wellness, a fully virtual practice serving adolescents, adults and older adults throughout Florida. She holds a master's degree in psychiatric and mental health nursing from Wilkes University and a Bachelor of Science in Nursing from Georgia Southern University. Her practice is at purpleelephantpsych.com.
