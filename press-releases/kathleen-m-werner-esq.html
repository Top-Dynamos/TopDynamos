Kathleen M. Werner, Esq., Marks More Than Three Decades of Advocacy and Leadership in Personal Injury Law

Owner of The Dorsey Law Firm Kathleen M. Werner, Esq., has built a distinguished legal career focused on personal injury, automobile accident and workers' compensation matters while maintaining an extensive commitment to professional and community service.

The Dorsey Law FirmLeonardtown, MDAugust 5, 2026

LEONARDTOWN, Md.: The Dynamo Times highlights Kathleen M. Werner, Esq., for more than three decades of dedication to personal injury law, client advocacy, legal leadership and community service.

Ms. Werner is the owner of The Dorsey Law Firm in Leonardtown, Maryland, where she has built a career focused on representing individuals navigating difficult circumstances. Her practice includes automobile accident and workers' compensation matters: areas of law in which clients may be dealing simultaneously with physical injuries, financial uncertainty, insurance issues and disruption to their everyday lives.

For Ms. Werner, practicing law has developed into a career that extends beyond legal procedure. It involves helping people understand their options and advocating for their interests at moments when experienced representation can be especially important.

Ms. Werner's professional journey with The Dorsey Law Firm spans more than three decades. She joined the firm as an associate attorney in 1992, and over the years her responsibilities and leadership continued to grow. In 2003, she became a partner in the firm. Then, in 2024, Ms. Werner assumed ownership of The Dorsey Law Firm: a progression from associate attorney to partner and ultimately owner that reflects a longstanding relationship with the firm and the community it serves.

Personal injury law frequently begins with an unexpected event. An automobile accident can change someone's circumstances within seconds. A workplace injury can affect an individual's health, income and ability to perform his or her job. Clients facing these situations may suddenly find themselves dealing with medical appointments, insurers, employers and legal questions at the same time. Ms. Werner's practice focuses on helping clients navigate these circumstances, combining legal knowledge with the ability to understand the practical challenges confronting injured individuals.

Effective representation in these situations requires careful attention to facts, documentation, deadlines and communication, and also compassion. For clients, a legal matter may be one case among many within the broader justice system. For the individual experiencing it, however, the situation can affect nearly every part of everyday life.

Ms. Werner developed an early interest in public service and advocacy. She attended Mary Washington College, now known as the University of Mary Washington, where she earned a Bachelor of Arts in American Studies and Political Science. During college, she gained exposure to government and public service through an internship with United States Senator Paul Sarbanes in 1984. She subsequently earned her Juris Doctor from the University of Baltimore School of Law in 1989.

Her career developed from law clerk in 1991 to associate attorney, partner and eventually law firm owner. Becoming the owner of a law firm requires more than practicing law: it also involves leadership. A firm owner must consider clients, employees, operations, professional standards and the long-term direction of the practice. Ms. Werner's assumption of ownership in 2024 therefore represents both a legal and entrepreneurial milestone.

Ms. Werner has remained active within the broader legal profession. Her professional involvement includes the Maryland State Bar Association and the Maryland Association for Justice. Since 2018, she has also served as a Trustee for the Client Protection Fund of the Bar of Maryland: an important role in maintaining confidence in the legal profession by addressing qualifying losses caused by attorney misconduct.

Ms. Werner's record of service extends substantially beyond her law practice. She serves on the St. Mary's County Adult Public Guardianship Review Board and has held leadership roles as president of the St. Mary's Animal Welfare League and president of the St. Mary's County League of Women Voters. Her civic involvement has also encompassed the St. Mary's County Commission for Women, Tri County Youth Services Bureau, Teen Center Advisory Board, St. Mary's County Cable Advisory Committee, and the St. Mary's County Metropolitan Commission, where she served as both vice chair and chair.

In 2020, Ms. Werner was named among Maryland's Top 100 Women by The Daily Record. The recognition reflected accomplishments extending across her professional career and community involvement. While individual honors mark important milestones, Ms. Werner's broader professional story is defined by consistency: more than three decades in law, more than three decades connected with the same firm, years of involvement in legal organizations, and sustained participation in civic and community service.

The legal profession has changed significantly since Ms. Werner began practicing. Technology has transformed legal research and communication, clients have greater access to information, and the regulatory and insurance environments surrounding personal injury and workers' compensation matters continue to evolve. Ms. Werner attributes part of her professional success to resilience and adaptability developed while growing up in a Navy family, characteristics that have remained relevant throughout a career involving changes in both law and leadership.

Assuming ownership of The Dorsey Law Firm in 2024 opened a new chapter in Ms. Werner's career. Her stated focus remains on the continued growth and success of the practice while maintaining the professionalism and compassion that have characterized her work. Clients need knowledgeable representation, but they also need attorneys who understand that the legal matter often accompanies a difficult period in their lives.

The Dynamo Times recognizes Kathleen M. Werner, Esq., for her longstanding contributions to personal injury law and for a career characterized by advocacy, professional leadership, community involvement and service.

About The Dorsey Law Firm

Kathleen M. Werner, Esq., is the owner of The Dorsey Law Firm in Leonardtown, Maryland, and has more than three decades of experience in personal injury law. She joined The Dorsey Law Firm as an associate attorney in 1992, became a partner in 2003 and assumed ownership of the firm in 2024. Her practice focuses on automobile accident and workers' compensation matters. Ms. Werner earned a Bachelor of Arts in American Studies and Political Science from Mary Washington College and her Juris Doctor from the University of Baltimore School of Law. She is involved with the Maryland State Bar Association and Maryland Association for Justice and has served as a Trustee for the Client Protection Fund of the Bar of Maryland since 2018.
