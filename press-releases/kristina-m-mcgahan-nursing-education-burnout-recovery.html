Kristina M. McGahan, RN, BSN, Advances Nursing Education, Cardiovascular Care and Burnout Recovery

Registered nurse, nurse consultant, recovery coach and owner Kristina M. McGahan, RN, BSN, draws on more than 28 years in health care to strengthen clinical education while helping nurses address workplace stress, burnout and professional resilience.

KMM Consulting / Mind Over Matter: Nursing Recovery SummitMagnolia, TXAugust 18, 2026

MAGNOLIA, Texas: Dynamo Times highlights Kristina M. McGahan, RN, BSN, an experienced registered nurse whose career spans more than 28 years across cardiovascular care, telemetry, progressive care, medical-surgical nursing, nursing education and professional wellness.

McGahan's career brings together two closely connected areas of health care: caring for patients and supporting the nurses responsible for delivering that care. Her professional work includes experience as a nurse consultant, recovery coach and clinical nurse coordinator for a cardiac care unit. Over the course of her career, she has also served in leadership, educator and preceptor roles, helping orient and mentor nurses while supporting high standards of clinical practice.

More recently, McGahan has expanded her work into nurse wellness and burnout recovery. Drawing in part from her own professional experiences, she founded Mind Over Matter: Nursing Recovery Summit, an initiative designed to help nurses process workplace stress and trauma, develop resilience and reconnect with their professional purpose. The Inner Circle has recognized McGahan as a Pinnacle Lifetime Member for her contributions to nursing education and burnout recovery.

McGahan earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing from The University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston in 1998. Her professional credentials include Registered Nurse status as well as Advanced Cardiac Life Support, Basic Life Support and NIH Stroke Scale certification. Her clinical education has continued throughout her career as she has worked in cardiovascular and hospital-based nursing environments.

Cardiac nursing requires careful observation and a strong understanding of cardiovascular function. Telemetry adds another layer by allowing clinicians to continuously monitor electrical activity of the heart. Nurses working in this environment must understand what they are seeing, recognize concerning changes and communicate effectively when intervention may be required. McGahan has developed extensive experience in cardiac and telemetry care throughout her career, and that knowledge has become an important part of her educational work with other nurses.

In October 2025, McGahan expanded her educational work through publication of What Every Nurse Must Know: Telemetry Secrets. The book was developed as a practical resource for nursing professionals seeking to strengthen their telemetry knowledge and clinical confidence. Telemetry can be intimidating for nurses who are new to cardiac monitoring: patterns on a monitor must be connected to an understanding of cardiac physiology and the patient's overall clinical presentation. For McGahan, authorship represents another extension of the educator role she has held throughout her nursing career.

McGahan has served in preceptor, educator and leadership roles within hospital systems. Preceptors help newer nurses translate academic knowledge into real clinical practice: they teach procedures, reinforce standards, answer questions and model professional judgment. McGahan's work has included contributing to orientation programs and mentoring nurses as they adapt to demanding clinical environments.

Health care professionals routinely care for people during some of the most difficult moments of their lives. That responsibility can carry a substantial emotional and physical burden. Long shifts, staffing pressures, high-acuity patients, workplace trauma and the expectation to remain constantly focused can accumulate over time. McGahan's work increasingly focuses on what happens to the caregiver under those conditions. Drawing from her own experiences with professional burnout, she developed a mission centered on helping nurses recognize and address the effects of chronic workplace stress.

McGahan founded Mind Over Matter: Nursing Recovery Summit as an initiative focused specifically on nurses experiencing professional stress and burnout. Through recovery coaching and educational programming, McGahan works with nurses seeking to rebuild balance, confidence and career satisfaction. Her work reflects an important idea: supporting the health care workforce can ultimately support patient care as well.

McGahan has received Nurse of the Year honors in both 2021 and 2022 from Christus Santa Rosa Hospital. She was also recognized with a Woman of Empowerment Award in 2025. McGahan has credited nurses Natalie Jordy, RN, and Jana England as important mentors in her professional journey: a recognition that parallels the role she has taken on for other nurses.

Dynamo Times recognizes Kristina M. McGahan, RN, BSN, for her contributions to cardiovascular nursing, nursing education, professional mentorship and nurse burnout recovery.

About KMM Consulting / Mind Over Matter: Nursing Recovery Summit

Kristina M. McGahan, RN, BSN, is a registered nurse, nurse consultant, recovery coach and nursing educator with more than 28 years of health care experience. Her clinical background includes cardiovascular care, cardiac and telemetry nursing, progressive care and medical-surgical nursing. She earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing from The University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston in 1998 and maintains professional credentials including RN, ACLS, BLS and NIH Stroke Scale certification. McGahan is the founder of Mind Over Matter: Nursing Recovery Summit and the author of What Every Nurse Must Know: Telemetry Secrets.
