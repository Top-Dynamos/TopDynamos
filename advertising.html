Advertising

What we sell, what we decline, and where the line sits between a commercial arrangement and editorial judgement.

Last updated October 16, 2026

01Who Reads The Dynamo Times

The Dynamo Times covers the professional record of executives, physicians, attorneys, academics, engineers, founders and public servants, across business and leadership, health, law, finance, education, and science and technology.

Readers arrive through search, through the people we feature, and through the organisations those people work for. It is a narrow audience rather than a broad one, and that is the point.

02What Is Available

  • Paid placements. A press release or professional profile, researched, written and published by our desk, carrying a visible paid label. This is the main thing we sell.
  • Display placements. Fixed positions on section and article pages.
  • Category sponsorship. Association with one of our industry sections for an agreed period.

Every commercial placement is labelled on the page. Our Sponsored Content Policy sets out exactly how.

03Rates and Availability

Rates depend on the format, the section and the period, so we quote rather than publish a price list.

Write to admin@topdynamos.com with what you have in mind and we will come back to you with options and current availability. If it is easier to talk, telephone +1 (631) 824-0352.

04What We Will Not Run

We turn work away. The list is short and it is firm.

  • Anything that makes a claim we cannot support, including unverifiable credentials, awards or figures.
  • Anything whose purpose is to attack a named competitor or individual.
  • Anything about a person who has not agreed to be featured.
  • Advertising for products or services that are unlawful, or that we judge likely to mislead or harm our readers.
  • Placements designed to be mistaken for independent editorial, or any request to remove the paid label.
  • Any arrangement conditional on a guaranteed search ranking, traffic figure or placement inside an AI assistant. Nobody can honestly guarantee those, so we do not.

05Commercial and Editorial Are Separate

Buying a placement does not give you influence over our editorial coverage, over how a fact about you is stated, or over whether a correction is published. Our Editorial Policy covers this in full.

Equally, declining to buy anything does not affect whether we cover you. We do not use coverage as leverage.

06Supplying Materials

For a paid placement, the more you give us the better the result. Useful material includes a current biography, employer and title, dates, named awards with the awarding body, and a photograph.

On photographs: supply the highest resolution original you have, and give us room around the head rather than a tightly cropped headshot. We crop to fit our layout. We cannot add back what a crop has already removed, and a small image cannot be made sharp by enlarging it.

By supplying material you confirm you are entitled to supply it and that any person featured in it agrees. See our Terms of Use.
