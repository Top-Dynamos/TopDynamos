Sponsored Content Policy

Much of what we publish is paid for. Here is how it is labelled, and the list of things money does not buy.

Last updated October 16, 2026

01Why This Page Exists

Much of what The Dynamo Times publishes is paid placement. Individuals and organisations pay for a press release or a professional profile to be written and published.

A reader cannot judge what they are reading unless they know that. This page sets out what paid material is, how it is marked, and what the money does and does not change. Our Editorial Policy covers the wider question of how we decide what to publish.

02The Three Categories

Paid placement

A press release or professional profile commissioned and paid for by the person featured or by someone acting for them. We write it, we check the claims in it, and we publish it. This is the largest category on the site.

Independent editorial

Material we commission ourselves, with no payment from anyone featured in it.

Advertising

Display or promotional placements sold as advertising, which are not written by our editorial desk. See the Advertising page.

03How Paid Material Is Marked

Paid material carries a visible label on the page itself, not only in a policy page a reader has to go looking for. The label appears near the top of the article, before the body text, in plain language a reader will understand: Paid Placement, or Sponsored.

We do not use labels designed to blur the line. Nothing on this site is marked with vague phrases such as partner content, brand voice or promoted insight in place of saying it was paid for.

This labelling requirement does not depend on who asks. It is not waived because a client would prefer it were.

04What Payment Buys

  • Consideration of the submission.
  • The work of researching, writing and editing the piece.
  • Publication on this site, and a permanent page.
  • A reasonable number of factual revisions after publication.

05What Payment Does Not Buy

  • A claim we cannot support. If a credential, award, figure or date cannot be verified, it does not go in, whoever is paying.
  • Invented praise. We do not assert that someone is leading, foremost or best. If a named body said it, we attribute it to that body.
  • Removal of a correction. Once a correction is warranted it is published and the note stays.
  • The label coming off. Paid material stays marked as paid material, permanently.
  • Coverage of someone who did not agree to it. We do not accept payment to write about a third party who has not consented.
  • Attack pieces. We do not accept payment for material whose purpose is to damage a named competitor or individual.
  • Search engine guarantees. We do not promise any particular ranking, traffic figure or placement in search results or AI assistants. Nobody can honestly promise that.

06Paid Does Not Mean Unchecked

Paid material is held to the same verification standard as anything else we publish. Credentials, titles, dates, employers and awards are taken from the person, their employer, a professional registry or a public record, and written as those sources state them.

If a client supplies a claim we cannot support, we go back to them and ask for a source. If there is no source, the claim comes out. That conversation happens before publication, not after a reader complains.

07Questions

If you are reading an article and you cannot tell whether it was paid for, that is a labelling failure on our part and we want to know. Write to admin@topdynamos.com with the link and we will check it and fix the label.
