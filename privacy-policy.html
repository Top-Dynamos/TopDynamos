Privacy Policy

What we collect, what we do not, what happens to the information of the people we write about, and how to make us change or remove something.

Last updated October 16, 2026

01Who We Are

The Dynamo Times is a professional news publication covering leadership, business, health, law, finance, education, and science and technology. The publication is operated by Top Dynamos, which is the party responsible for the personal information described on this page. Where the law uses the term data controller, Top Dynamos is the data controller.

You can reach us about anything on this page at admin@topdynamos.com or by telephone at +1 (631) 824-0352.

This policy covers thedynamotimes.com. It does not cover other websites we link to, including the personal and company websites of people we write about. Those sites set their own terms.

02What We Collect, and What We Do Not

We have deliberately kept this short, because the site itself is simple. There is no account to create, no login, no comment system, no paywall, and no advertising network.

Information you give us

  • Submissions. If you use the Submit News form, we receive the name, email address, organisation, headline, summary and any notes you enter. If you email or telephone us instead, we receive whatever you choose to put in that message.
  • Newsletter. If you enter an email address in the newsletter field in our footer, we receive that address.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. Our host records standard web server information when a page is requested, including the IP address, the time, the page requested, and the browser user agent. We do not control the retention of these logs and we do not use them to build profiles of visitors.
  • Analytics, only if you accept. If you accept analytics in our cookie banner, a measurement script records page views and clicks on links and buttons. If you decline, or simply ignore the banner, the script is never loaded and no analytics cookie is set.

What we never collect

  • We do not ask for, store or process payment card details anywhere on this site.
  • We do not run behavioural advertising, retargeting pixels or advertising networks.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined under California law.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children. See the section on children below.

03People We Write About

This is the part of our privacy practice that matters most, and it is the part most privacy policies leave out.

The Dynamo Times publishes press releases and professional profiles about named, identifiable people. That material routinely includes a person name, photograph, job title, employer, professional history, credentials, and the public achievements that made them newsworthy. All of it is personal information, and publishing it is a processing activity.

Where that information comes from

  • Material supplied to us directly by the person featured or by someone acting for them.
  • Material supplied under a paid placement or recognition arrangement.
  • Publicly available professional sources, including published press releases, employer biographies, professional registries and public records.

Why we are allowed to publish it

Where the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on consent where the person supplied the material themselves, and otherwise on our legitimate interest in publishing professional journalism about people in public professional life, balanced against the interests of the person concerned. Journalistic processing also carries specific exemptions in several jurisdictions.

If you are featured and want something changed

Write to admin@topdynamos.com. Tell us the page, the sentence, and what is wrong. We treat factual corrections seriously and handle them under our Corrections Policy. We will not quietly alter the record: a material correction is noted on the page.

We will also consider a request to remove a page entirely. We do not promise in advance to grant one. Removing accurate published journalism is not something a publication should do on request alone, so we weigh the request against the public interest in the material remaining available, and we tell you the outcome either way.

05How We Use Information

  • To consider and publish submissions. If you send us a submission, we read it, we may contact you about it, and we may publish material based on it.
  • To answer you. If you contact us, we use your details to reply.
  • To understand what gets read. If you accepted analytics, aggregate page view and click counts tell us which stories find an audience.
  • To keep the site working and secure. Server logs help diagnose faults and abuse.

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, we do not enrich it with data bought from brokers, and we do not feed it to automated decision making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

07Where Information Is Held

We operate from the United States and our providers are principally based in the United States. If you are in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, information relating to you will be transferred to and processed in the United States, which has not received an adequacy decision of general application.

Our providers make these transfers under Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent approved mechanism in their own terms. If you want to know what applies to a specific provider, write to us and we will tell you what we know.

08How Long We Keep It

  • Submissions and correspondence. Kept while we are considering the submission and afterwards as an editorial record, since we may need to show where published material came from. We review and clear material we no longer need.
  • Newsletter addresses. Kept until you ask to be removed.
  • Analytics. Held by our analytics provider under its own retention schedule. We only see aggregate figures.
  • Server logs. Held by our host under its retention schedule, which we do not control.
  • Published articles and profiles. Kept indefinitely as part of the published record, subject to the removal requests described above.

09Your Rights

If you are in the UK, EU or Switzerland

You have the right to ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to have inaccurate information corrected, to ask for erasure, to ask us to restrict how we use it, to object to processing we carry out on the basis of legitimate interests, and to receive certain information in a portable form. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time, and withdrawing it does not affect anything done beforehand.

Some of these rights are narrowed where information is processed for journalism. We will tell you plainly if that is the reason we cannot do what you asked.

You also have the right to complain to your national supervisory authority, and in the United Kingdom to the Information Commissioner Office.

If you are in California

You have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request a copy, to request correction, and to request deletion. You have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross context behavioural advertising, so there is no sale or sharing for you to opt out of.

If you are elsewhere in the United States

Several states now provide rights comparable to those above. We apply the same process to every request we receive, wherever you live, rather than asking you to prove which law applies.

How to exercise them

Email admin@topdynamos.com with the subject line Privacy Request. Tell us what you want and enough detail to find your information. We will respond within thirty days, or tell you why we need longer. We may need to confirm who you are before we act, which protects you more than it protects us. You may use an authorised agent.

10Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Form submissions travel over encrypted connections. Access to the inbox that receives submissions is limited to the people who need it.

We will not claim this makes anything unbreakable, because no one can honestly say that. If we become aware of a breach affecting personal information, we will notify the people affected and the relevant authorities where the law requires it.

11Children

The Dynamo Times is a professional publication intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under sixteen. If you believe a child has sent us information, write to admin@topdynamos.com and we will delete it.

12Changes to This Policy

When our practices change, this page changes with them, and the date at the top changes too. If a change materially affects how we handle your information, we will say so clearly on this page rather than relying on you to spot the new date.

This policy describes what the site actually does at the date shown. If you find something on this page that does not match what you observe, tell us. That is a fault worth hearing about.
